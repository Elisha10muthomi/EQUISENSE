About

Built for the
Everyday Investor

EquiSense is a knowledge-based expert system developed to bridge the gap between complex institutional trading tools and the everyday retail investor who needs simple, transparent and explainable investment guidance.

Unlike black-box machine learning systems, EquiSense uses a structured rule-based engine that mirrors the reasoning of a financial analyst — making every recommendation fully traceable and understandable.
